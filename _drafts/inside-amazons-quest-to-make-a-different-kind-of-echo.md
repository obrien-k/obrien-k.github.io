Amazon making another Echo

> Of [all the devices Amazon announced last fall](https://www.engadget.com/2017/09/28/amazon-echo-event/), the one that got the most attention was the [Echo Spot](https://www.engadget.com/2017/12/18/amazon-echo-spot-review/). A smaller, spherical version of the [Echo Show](https://www.engadget.com/2017/06/26/amazon-echo-show-review/), the Spot exuded an adorable style that was distinct from the rest of the Echo lineup. And judging by the reviews it received, it’s clear that the Spot’s form factor helped make it something of a surprise hit. In an exclusive behind-the-scenes look, Engadget had a chance to find out just how the Spot became one of Amazon’s most unique products.
